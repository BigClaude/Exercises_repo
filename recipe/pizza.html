Italian Pizza



Description

1. Description Italian pizza is a traditional Italian dish made from a thin,
soft or crispy dough topped with tomato sauce, mozzarella cheese,
olive oil, and fresh herbs. It is baked in a hot oven until
the crust is golden and the cheese melts.
It is commonly served for lunch or dinner.

Ingredients

  • 500 g wheat flour
  • 300 ml warm water
  • g dry yeast
  • teaspoon salt

Preparation Steps

  1. Make the dough: Add flour, salt, and olive oil. Mix and knead for about 8–10 minutes until the dough
  2. Let the dough rise: Cover the dough and leave it in a warm place for 1–2 hours, or until it doubles in size.
  3. Preheat the oven: Heat the oven to its highest setting, ideally 240–250°C.
  4. Shape the dough: Divide the dough into portions and roll each portion into a thin, round pizza base.
  5. Add the toppings: Spread tomato sauce over the base. Add mozzarella cheese, sliced tomatoes
